# Pass E: information for a participant (draft, for your review)

> **What this is:** the sheet a participant reads, or has read to them, before a pass E session (`pass-e-procedure.md` §3). It is a draft: you review it before anyone sees it. Whether a session also needs written consent is your decision (§7 there). The bracketed parts are yours to fill in.

---

## Reading some drawings

**What this is for.** We are making drawings, some of them moving, that are meant to show short pieces of text. We want to know what the drawings say to someone who hasn't seen the texts. This tests the drawings, not you. There are no right or wrong answers.

**What happens.**
1. You see some drawings, one at a time, on a screen. Some move.
2. For each one, you say or write what you see happening. You can take as long as you like, replay a drawing that moves, or move one that can be turned.
3. Then you see the texts the drawings were made from, and for each drawing you choose the text you think it shows.

It takes about [how long].

**What the drawings are about.** Everyday situations and a few topics from school and from planning work. Some texts describe a feeling, such as embarrassment or worry, in an ordinary situation. You can skip any drawing without saying why.

**What is kept.**
- The page you use keeps nothing and sends nothing anywhere. When the session ends it is cleared.
- [The facilitator] writes down what you say about each drawing, word for word, and which text you chose for it.
- Your name and anything else that could identify you are not written down.
- What you say may be quoted, without your name, in notes about how well the drawings work.

**You can stop at any time,** without giving a reason. If you stop, [what happens to what was written down so far, for example: it is not used].

**Questions:** ask [the facilitator] before, during or after the session.
