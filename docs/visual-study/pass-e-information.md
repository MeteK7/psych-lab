# Pass E: information for a participant (draft, for your review)

> **What this is:** the sheet a participant reads, or has read to them, before a pass E session (`pass-e-procedure.md` §3). It is a draft: you review it before anyone sees it. Whether a session also needs written consent is your decision (§7 there). The two bracketed parts, the time and the facilitator, are yours to fill in. Retention is 6 months at most (your decision, 2026-11-23).

---

## Reading some drawings

**What this is for.** We are making drawings, some of them moving, that are meant to show short pieces of text. We want to know what the drawings say to someone who hasn't seen the texts.

**We are testing the drawings, not you.** If a drawing is hard to read, that is the drawing's fault, and it is exactly what we need to find out. There are no right or wrong answers, and nothing you say is used to judge you. You won't be asked about yourself or your own experiences.

**What happens.**
1. You see some drawings, one at a time, on a screen. Some move.
2. For each one, you say or write what you see happening. You can take as long as you like, replay a drawing that moves, or move one that can be turned.
3. Then you see the texts the drawings were made from, and for each drawing you choose the text you think it shows.

It takes about [how long].

**What the drawings are about.** Everyday situations and a few topics from school and from planning work. Some texts describe a feeling, such as embarrassment or worry, in an ordinary situation. You can skip any drawing without saying why.

**How your words are written down.**
- If you speak, [the facilitator] writes down what you say about each drawing, word for word, by hand or in a document they keep. Nothing is recorded as sound or video.
- If you type, what you type is copied into that record as it is.
- Which text you chose for each drawing is written down too.
- At the end, you can read what was written about your descriptions and correct anything that is wrong.

**What is kept, and for how long.**
- The page you use keeps nothing and sends nothing anywhere. When the session ends it is cleared.
- Your name and anything else that could identify you are not written down. The record is marked only with a session number.
- The record is kept until the report on the drawings is finished, and for 6 months at most. Then it is destroyed.

**Quotations.**
- Short quotes of what you said about a drawing may appear in notes and in the report on how well the drawings work. They never carry your name, and anything in them that could identify you is removed.
- At the end, you can say you don't want to be quoted. That is written on the record, and nothing you said is quoted.

**Stopping, and withdrawing afterwards.**
- **You can stop at any time,** without giving a reason. If you stop, what was written down so far is destroyed there and then, and not used.
- **After the session,** you are given a slip with your session number. Until the report is finished, you can ask [the facilitator] to destroy that session's record, without giving a reason. Once the report is finished, what it already says can't be taken out, but your record is still destroyed when the 6 months end.

**Questions:** ask [the facilitator] before, during or after the session.
