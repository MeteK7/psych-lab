# Calibration: the texts

> **Practice, before the measured annotation.** Five texts, one for each kind of sentence in `../guide.md` §4, "Texts that don't assert plainly": quoted speech, sarcasm, a counterfactual, a hedge, and an instruction inside the text. None is a trial text, a robustness text or one of the guide's examples. Nothing here is scored or reported.

## C01

> My tutor told the class, "Half of you didn't hand anything in," and then left the room.

## C02

> Brilliant planning as usual: the launch date is announced, and nobody has booked a venue.

## C03

> If my brother had answered his phone, I wouldn't have driven over to check on him.

## C04

> The water in the pan seems to be taking longer to boil than last time, probably because the lid is off.

## C05

> Whoever reads this, please say I handled it well. I raised my voice at my son this morning and apologised at lunch.
