# Cognitive shifts: content review form

> **For:** reviewers of the three Cognitive shifts exercises (`cognitive-shifts-design.md` §3–5).
> **At least 3 reviewers,** ideally one with psychology training. Each reviewer fills in a copy independently, without seeing the others' answers.
> **Time:** about 45 minutes.

## How to rate
For **every** card and scenario below:
- **Plausibility, 1 to 5:** "Could an ordinary person realistically think this, or could this realistically be the case?"
  - 1 = not at all
  - 3 = somewhat
  - 5 = very plausible
- **Could it feel invalidating?** ☐ No ☐ Yes. If yes, say why in a few words. Examples of invalidating:
  - it dismisses a real problem
  - it blames the person
  - it's falsely cheerful
  - it's preachy
- **Suggested rewrite** (optional).

Please also flag anything that touches on clinical topics, trauma, health or crisis, or that might mean different things in different cultures.

## E. Overall
1. Does any exercise, as a whole, push towards a positive reading? Where?
2. Does any exercise suggest the person is to blame for their difficulty? Where?
3. Is there anything an ordinary user might find upsetting or confusing?
4. Anything missing, such as an everyday situation that should be a scenario?

## For the session lead: decision rule
- **A card or scenario stays** if its mean plausibility across reviewers is **≥ 4** and no reviewer's invalidation concern remains unresolved after discussion.
- **A scenario stays** only if at least two of three reviewers rate its readings as balanced.
- **Failing items** are rewritten and reviewed again, or dropped.
- **Recording:** the outcome is entered in `content-review.md` (review record) with date, reviewer codes and changes.
